## The short answer on channels

Email works for **two of the four stops**: Burdock and Kobo. Phone works for **two**:
the Cascade Room and Uncle Abe's. Two of those channels are worse than they look. **Kobo
has no phone at all, anywhere**, and the only number on its site is a placeholder. **The
Cascade Room has no email at all**, so its questions go through the phone or the Tock
form. For Uncle Abe's the phone number comes from the venue's page, because the database
row has none.

Two of the confirmations need a person rather than a document: whether the Burdock
counter seats a walk-in at 18:30, and whether the Kobo shop has seats inside.

## Notes on the data, for whoever fixes the rows

- **Uncle Abe's** and **Kobo Ice Cream** have no phone in the database. Uncle Abe's
  number is on the venue's own page; Kobo's is nowhere.
- **Kobo Ice Cream** is stored with a double space in the name. It prints as Kobo Ice
  Cream in the route.
- **Kobo Ice Cream's** hours column carries two seasonal rules with no field saying
  which is current. A route that reads the column without picking a rule can put the
  shop at times it is shut.
