# Mount Pleasant: coffee roasted here, beer brewed here

**Route:** 5 stops · 3 categories · 900m walked · 09:00 to late · runs Monday to
Saturday.

Hours verified from the database on 2026-11-02. Venue facts verified from the

---

Five stops between Ontario Street and Main: coffee at the roastery, pastry on 10th,
lunch on Main, a brewery tasting room at 14:30, Spanish small plates on 7th at 17:00
[[s1]]. 900m on foot, no transport.

### 09:00 — Elysian Coffee Roasters
2301 Ontario Street · Cafe · Mon–Fri 07:00–18:00, Sat–Sun 08:00–18:00

The company lists this address as its roastery and tasting room, at 7th and Ontario,
one of four Elysian cafés in Vancouver [[s2]]. Beans are sold as espresso and filter, and
by subscription, through the same site [[s3]].

*If it is shut or full:* **Modus**, 242m, open 08:00–16:00 weekdays [[s1]]. **Milano Cafe**,
282m, open 07:00–18:00 daily [[s1]].

### 10:30 — Thierry
265 East 10th Avenue · Cafe · Mon–Thu 08:00–21:00, Fri–Sat 08:00–22:00

Thierry Busset is a maître artisan chocolatier from Auvergne, and the Mount Pleasant
shop is one of three [[s4]]. The counter runs pastries, cakes and tarts, macarons,
chocolates and canapés, with a breakfast and lunch menu behind it [[s5]].

*If it is shut or full:* **Bean Around The World**, 71m, open 07:30–21:30 weekdays [[s1]].
**Trees**, 99m, open 07:00–21:00 weekdays [[s1]].

### 12:00 — Barney's On Main
2526 Main Street · Restaurant · Mon–Thu 10:00–22:00, Fri 10:00–24:00, Sat 09:00–24:00,
Sun 09:00–22:00

The daytime menu runs to 15:30: two eggs, hash browns and toast $13, the same with
bacon, bangers, ham or avocado $16, avocado toast with two eggs $17 [[s7]]. A separate
weekend and holiday brunch card is published, so the weekday card may differ [[s8]].

*If it is shut or full:* **Ignite Pizzeria**, 42m, open 11:00–22:00 [[s1]]. **Tamaly Shop**,
43m, open 09:00–20:00 [[s1]]. **Sushi Kaido**, 67m, open 11:00–21:00 [[s1]].

### 14:30 — Main Street Brewing
261 East 7th Avenue · Bar / Pub · Mon–Thu 14:00–23:00, Fri–Sun 12:00–23:00

Happy hour in the tasting room runs 14:00–18:00 Tuesday to Sunday, and all day Monday:
16oz drafts at $6 and 20oz cask at $6.75, on the pilsner, the Naked Fox IPA, the
Kingpin pale ale and the Krush lager [[s9]]. Food comes off the tasting room menu [[s10]].

*If it is shut or full:* **Steamworks Brewing Co.**, 139m, open 11:30–23:00 Tuesday
to Thursday [[s1]].

### 17:00 — Como Taperia
201 East 7th Avenue · Restaurant · Daily 16:00–23:00

Spanish, in the Michelin Guide's Vancouver selection from 2022 through 2026, and one
of two Canadian restaurants holding the Restaurants from Spain certification [[s11]].
Reservations are for the inside room and walk-ins are welcome [[s12]]. The patio is
covered, unheated and seasonal, first come, first served while it is open [[s12]].

*If it is shut or full:* **Slim's BBQ**, 50m, open 11:30–01:00 Sunday to Wednesday [[s1]].
**The Pleasant**, 143m, open from 15:00 [[s1]].
