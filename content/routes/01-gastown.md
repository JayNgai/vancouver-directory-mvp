# Gastown: coffee to last call

**Published copy — version without unknowns.** Every time below was verified open on
a Tuesday from the database on 2026-11-02. Nothing claims a dish, a review or an
atmosphere the data does not hold.

**Route:** 5 stops · 3 categories · 692m walked · 10:00 to late · runs Tuesday to
Saturday.

---

Coffee at a roaster, lunch on Carrall, a chocolate stop while it is still open,
dinner back down the same street, and a bar that runs to two. Four blocks, one
afternoon, no transport.

### 10:00 — Timbertrain Coffee Roasters
311 West Cordova Street · Cafe · Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00, Sat–Sun 09:00–17:00

The first stop because it opens at eight. Roasts on site. Start on Cordova before
the street fills.

*If it is shut or full:* **Trees Organic Coffee**, 70m away, open 07:30–22:00 on
weekdays — the longer hours make it the safer swap. **Revolver**, 82m, opens 07:30
but closes at 18:00.

### 12:00 — Six Acres
203 Carrall Street · Bar / Pub · Sun–Thu 11:30–23:30, Fri–Sat 11:30–00:30

Lunch, and the turn onto Carrall that the rest of the day follows. Opens 11:30, so
noon needs no queue.

*If it is shut or full:* **The Portside Pub**, 45m, open from 11:00 daily.
**The Blarney Stone**, 67m, open from 11:00.

### 15:00 — East Van Roasters
319 Carrall Street · Cafe · Tue–Sat 10:00–17:00

The stop that fixes the route's days: it closes at 17:00 and is **shut Sunday and
Monday**, which is why this walk runs Tuesday to Saturday.

*If it is shut:* **Aiyaohno Cafe**, 103m, Tue–Sat 08:00–15:00 — note it closes at
15:00, so it only works if you are early. **Buro Coffee Bar**, 155m, open Tue–Fri
07:45–20:30.

### 17:00 — L'Abattoir
217 Carrall Street · Restaurant · Tue–Sat 17:00–23:00

Dinner at opening time. Two minutes back down Carrall from the chocolate stop.

*If it is shut or full:* **Oku Izakaya**, 35m, opens 17:00 — same slot, no gap.
**The Greek Gastown**, 7m, opens 11:30 and runs to 22:00.

### 20:00 — Guilt & Co
1 Alexander Street · Bar / Pub · daily 19:00–02:00

The last stop, and the only one that opens in the evening. Two minutes downhill to
Alexander.

*If it is full:* **The Portside Pub**, 20m, open from 11:00 daily. **Six Acres**,
39m, open until 23:30.

## Where every fact came from

Hours: OpenStreetMap `opening_hours`, merged into the database on 2026-11-02 and
recorded per row in `dataSources`. Unchanged for stops that already had hours.
Distances: measured from stored coordinates with the same haversine the concierge
uses. Alternatives: same category within 400m, filtered to those verified open at
the stop's time on a Tuesday.
