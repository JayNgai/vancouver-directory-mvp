doubts. Channels were read from each venue's own site on 2026-11-02.

**Channel:** phone +1-604-629-7562. **The website returns 401 to automated requests

### L'Abattoir — 217 Carrall
**Confirm:**

**Channel:** phone +1-604-568-1701 · **email info@labattoir.ca** · contact page at

### Guilt & Co — 1 Alexander
**Confirm:**

**Channel:** web contact page at /contact.html only. **No phone number and no email
are published on it**, and the programme calendar loads through a path its robots.txt
disallows. The route copy already tells readers to confirm with the venue, because
the programme changes night to night.

## The short answer on channels

**Email is not generally available.** Of five stops, one publishes an email
(L'Abattoir). Of eight replacements, one does (The Portside Pub). Everything else is
phone number or web contact form, and two venues — East Van Roasters and Guilt & Co
— offer neither an email nor, in Guilt & Co's case, a phone.

So: phone for Timbertrain, Six Acres, East Van Roasters, L'Abattoir and the
replacements that list one; **L'Abattoir by email** if that is easier; and **Guilt &
Co by walking in or its web form**, since nothing else is published.

Two of the confirmations can only be answered in person anyway — how full the room
is at 10:00 on a Tuesday, and whether the Six Acres patio is actually open in
November.
