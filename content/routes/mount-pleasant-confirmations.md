**Email works for four of the five stops**: Elysian, Barney's, Main Street Brewing and
Como Taperia all publish an address. **Thierry is phone only**, because its address is
obfuscated on the page.

Two of the confirmations can only be answered by a person at the venue rather than a
document: whether the Elysian room has seats at opening time, and whether Como's patio
is still up in November.
