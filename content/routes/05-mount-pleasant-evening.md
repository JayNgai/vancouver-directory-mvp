Four stops in Mount Pleasant, all on Main between the 2500 and 3000 blocks: a cocktail
at five, dinner at half past six, an ice cream cone at half past eight, then the last
room, which closes at two or three in the morning [[s1]]. 695m, no transport.

The walk goes south to the dining room, back up the block for the cone, then south
again for the last room: the ice cream shuts at nine or ten, the bar does not [[s1]].

Thursday to Monday are the only days all four are open at their listed hours. Burdock
closes Tuesday and Wednesday; the other three are open daily.

### 17:00 — The Cascade Room
2616 Main Street · Bar / Pub · Mon–Wed 16:00–23:00, Thu 16:00–24:00, Fri–Sat
16:00–01:00, Sun 16:00–23:00

Named for Brewery Creek, the name this neighbourhood carried when the original
Vancouver Brewery stood in it and brewed a flagship beer called Cascade. Opened in 2007
[[s2]]. The hour is set for happy hour: 16:00–18:00 Tuesday to Saturday, all day Monday
[[s2]], and on Sunday the happy-hour drink prices run from 16:00 to close [[s3]]. A 16oz
Main Street pilsner is $6, the 20oz $7, a cocktail on tap $10, a pitcher $25 [[s3]].
Snacks start at $7, hand-cut fries [[s3]].

*If it is shut or full:* **Sing Sing**, 113m, open 11:30–01:00 Sunday to Thursday and to
02:00 Friday and Saturday [[s1]]. **Key Party**, 304m, opens 17:00 and closes 01:00 Sunday
to Wednesday, 02:00 Thursday to Saturday [[s1]].

### 18:30 — Burdock & Co
2702 Main Street · Restaurant · Thu–Mon 17:00–22:00, closed Tuesday and Wednesday

Andrea Carlson opened it in 2013 [[s5]]. In 2022 it took a Michelin star, the first
Canadian restaurant owned and operated by a female chef to hold one [[s5]]. The dining
room serves seven courses at $175 a person [[s5]]. The bar and kitchen counter take
walk-ins: an abridged version of that menu, or dishes ordered one at a time [[s6]].
Corkage is $50 a bottle, one bottle per two guests [[s6]]. The kitchen closes at 22:00
[[s5]].

*If it is shut or full:* **Mali Thai Food**, 16m, open 16:00–22:00 Monday to Friday and
11:30–22:00 Saturday and Sunday [[s1]]. **Kaori Izakaya**, 337m, open 12:00–24:00 daily
[[s1]].

### 20:30 — Kobo Ice Cream
2541 Main Street · Dessert · Sep–May: Sun–Thu 14:00–21:00, Fri–Sat 14:00–22:00

Small-batch ice cream to family recipes, from local ingredients [[s7]]. The case runs
vanilla, pistachio, orange-chocolate chip, London Fog, tahini, roasted walnut and
caramelized banana, with rotating dairy-free and vegan flavours beside them [[s7]] [[s9]].

*If it is shut:* no second dessert room within 400m publishes hours [[s1]]. **Rollz**,
105m at 2685 Main, is the nearest, and its row carries no hours, so it is not offered as
a substitute [[s1]].

### 21:15 — Uncle Abe's
3032 Main Street · Bar / Pub · Mon–Thu 16:00–02:00, Fri 16:00–03:00, Sat 12:00–03:00,
Sun 12:00–02:00

The last room, and the latest close. A covered, heated patio runs all year; dogs are
allowed [[s10]]. No reservations, at any hour [[s10]]. Monarch Burger shares the space,
its counter open to 01:00 Thursday and Sunday and to 02:00 Friday and Saturday [[s11]].
Happy hour is 16:00–18:00 daily, and its drink prices are published; the route arrives
after it, so the happy-hour prices are not the prices at 21:15, and the regular list was
not read [[s10]].

*If it is full:* **The Watson**, 37m, open to 24:00 Monday to Thursday and Sunday, to
01:00 Friday and Saturday [[s1]]. **Sing Sing**, 287m, to 01:00 Sunday to Thursday and
02:00 Friday and Saturday [[s1]].

## Hour conflicts, recorded rather than resolved

- **The Cascade Room** prints two hour blocks on its own site that disagree [[s4]]. The
  block in the page body gives Thursday to 00:00 and Sunday to 23:00 [[s4]]. The footer
  block gives Thursday to 23:00 and Sunday to 22:00 [[s4]]. The database matches the
  body block.
  Scout Magazine gives Sunday to 22:00 and Friday and Saturday from 15:00, against the
  site's 16:00 [[s2]]. The stop is at 17:00, which is inside every reading, so the route
  holds and the doubt is printed rather than argued away [[s1]].
- **Kobo Ice Cream**: the hours column carries two seasonal rules, June to August and
  September to May, with no field saying which is current. The September–May rule
  matches the shop's own winter hours [[s8]]. The shop's FAQ page gives a third reading,
  open daily 12:00–22:00 [[s9]]. The stop is at 20:30, inside all three, so the route does
  not depend on which is right [[s1]].
- **Burdock & Co**: checked, no conflict. The database and the venue both say Thursday
  to Monday, 17:00–22:00, closed Tuesday and Wednesday [[s1]] [[s5]].
- **Uncle Abe's**: checked, no conflict. The database and the venue agree exactly, down
  to the 03:00 close on Friday and Saturday [[s1]].
