481m end to end. A patisserie at nine, a bakery counter at eleven, Cantonese barbecue
at half past twelve, a café at half past two, then Bao Bei's small plates at half past
five [[s1]].

Monday is the only day that does not work: Annabelle's and Chinatown BBQ are both shut.
Tuesday to Sunday are the days all five are open.

### 09:00 — Annabelle's
620 Quebec Street · Cafe · Tue–Fri 08:00–16:00, Sat–Sun 09:00–16:00

A café, patisserie and gelato shop run by Annabelle Choi, in the room that was Say
Hello Sweets until it closed at the end of February 2024 [[s2]]. The pastry case is
house-made. Gelato is the other half of the offer.

*If it is shut:* **Dalina**, 142m, open 07:00–17:00 daily, the fourth stop on this
route taken out of order [[s1]].

### 11:00 — New Town Bakery & Restaurant
148 East Pender Street · Restaurant · Mon–Tue and Thu–Sun 06:30–20:30, Wed
06:30–19:30

Family-run since 1980, and the Chinatown shop is the original [[s3]]. The case holds
apple tarts and steamed buns (siopao) in BBQ pork asado and chicken deluxe bola-bola
[[s3]]. The last order is at 18:45 [[s4]].

*If it is shut or full:* **Golden Smell Mee**, 7m, open 11:00–20:00 Monday to
Wednesday [[s1]]. **El Torito**, 74m, open 10:00–22:00 Tuesday to Thursday [[s1]].
**Juke**, 123m, open 11:00–24:00 daily [[s1]].

### 12:30 — Chinatown BBQ
130 East Pender Street · Restaurant · Tue–Sun 11:00–20:00, closed Monday

Cantonese barbecue, three ways: honey-glazed BBQ pork, crisp-skinned roast pork and
soy-sauce chicken [[s5]]. The dining room is open again after a closure [[s5]].

*If it is shut or full:* **DD Mau Chinatown**, 56m, open 11:30–22:00 Monday to
Thursday [[s1]]. **Virtuous Pie**, 129m, open 11:30–22:00 Sunday to Thursday [[s1]].

### 14:30 — Dalina
687 Main Street · Cafe · Daily 07:00–17:00

Breakfast and lunch [[s6]]. It shuts at 17:00, half an hour before Bao Bei opens [[s1]].

*If it is shut:* **Annabelle's**, 142m, open until 16:00 Tuesday to Friday [[s1]].

### 17:30 — Bao Bei
163 Keefer Street · Restaurant · Sun–Thu 17:30–22:00, Fri–Sat 17:30–24:00

Small plates for sharing, in the restaurant's own words, plus a family-table option for
a group [[s7]]. Dinner only, from 17:30 [[s1]].

*If it is shut or full:* **Juke**, 36m, open 11:00–24:00 daily [[s1]]. **DD Mau Chinatown**,
102m, open to 22:00 Monday to Thursday [[s1]]. **Between 2 Buns**, 144m, open 16:00–21:00
Tuesday to Thursday [[s1]].

## Why the route ends at dinner

The district has three bars with published hours, and none of them sits within 400m of
another: The Keefer Bar is 518m from Alibi Room, and Alibi Room is 1017m from The
Ivanhoe Pub, on the stored coordinates [[s8]]. The Keefer Bar is 55m from Bao Bei and
opens at 16:00, closing at 01:00 Sunday to Thursday and 02:00 on Friday and Saturday,
on the database's hours [[s8]]. It is not a stop because a bar stop needs a second bar
within 400m that is verifiably open at the same hour, and there is none [[s8]]. The
Keefer Bar is named here so the option is not lost.
