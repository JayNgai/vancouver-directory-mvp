# Chinatown route — what to confirm

For Jay to work through before the route goes in. Ten questions plus one hour doubt.
Channels were read from each venue's own site on 2026-11-02.

## The stops

### Annabelle's — 620 Quebec
**Confirm:**
- Is the café still trading under this name? The database has hours, but there is no
  phone and no website on the row, and the last published coverage read is from 2024.
- What is in the pastry case and the gelato case on a Tuesday morning?
- Can a group of four sit down at 09:00?

**Channel:** **neither a phone nor an email is published anywhere we could read.**
The shop's only published channel is the Instagram account @annabellechoistudio, cited
in the Daily Hive piece. This stop has to be checked in person, or dropped.

### New Town Bakery & Restaurant — 148 East Pender
**Confirm:**

  bakery's own site says 19:30 seven days a week, with dine-in closing at 19:00 and the

**Channel:** phone +1-604-689-7835 · **email newtownbakery@gmail.com**. No contact form.

## The bar that is named but not a stop

**The Keefer Bar** — 133 Keefer, phone +1-604-688-1961, site thekeeferbar.com. It is
55m from Bao Bei and open 16:00 to 01:00 Sunday to Thursday, to 02:00 Friday and
Saturday, on the database's hours. It is not a stop because no second bar within 400m
publishes hours: Alibi Room and The Ivanhoe Pub both sit further away than that. If a
bar stop is wanted later, this is the venue to build it around.

## The short answer on channels

**Email is good in Chinatown.** Four of five stops publish an address — New Town,
Chinatown BBQ, Dalina and Bao Bei. The exception is **Annabelle's, which publishes no
phone and no email at all**, and that is the single weakest link in the three new
routes: if the shop cannot be reached or walked into, the whole 09:00 start needs
rebuilding around Dalina.

The Monday closure of Annabelle's and Chinatown BBQ is what sets the route's days. It
falls out of the database and is not in doubt; the only hours doubt is New Town's
closing time.
