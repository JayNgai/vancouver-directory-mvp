Kitsilano

A vibrant beach neighborhood known for its stunning sandy beach, fitness culture (home to many trainers), independent boutiques, and the historic Kitsilano Beach Hotel.

About

Kitsilano (meaning "bitter wind" in Squamish) is one of Vancouver's most iconic neighborhoods, home to Canada's largest public beach, the famous Kits Beach Pool, and a thriving fitness community.

Top Attractions

  • Kitsilano Beach: City's premier sandy beach with volleyball courts and swimming.
  • Kitsilano Beach Hotel: Historic Art Deco landmark.
  • Quilchena Aquarium: Local marine life exhibit.

Best Times to Visit

  • Summer Weekends: Beach activities and outdoor events
  • Morning: Beach yoga and fitness classes
  • Evenings: Waterfront dining and happy hour
