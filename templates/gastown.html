Gastown

Vancouver's oldest neighborhood, established in the 1860s, featuring cobblestone streets, Victorian architecture, and a thriving artisanal food scene.

Neighborhood Overview

Gastown is Vancouver's historic heart, built in the 1860s by Shipyards founder Duncan. Known for its preserved cobblestone streets, Victorian warehouses converted to trendy shops and restaurants, and the iconic Steam Clock that chimes every 15 minutes.

Top Attractions

The Steam Clock: Iconic landmark featuring a mechanical figure that sprays water every 15 minutes.
Pike Place Market Vancouver: Local farmers market with artisanal foods and crafts.
Coal Harbour Waterfront: Scenic walking path along False Creek.

Best Times to Visit

  • Weekends Morning: Farmers market (Saturdays) or craft fairs
  • Evenings: Dinner and drinks at gastropubs and wine bars
  • Year-Round: The Steam Clock operates 24/7

Tips for Visitors

  • Walk the cobblestone streets - they're charming but can be slippery when wet
  • Visit early morning to avoid crowds at popular restaurants
  • Grab coffee and watch the Steam Clock's water show
  • Combine with a visit to nearby Downtown for shopping
