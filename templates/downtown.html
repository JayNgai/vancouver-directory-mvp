Downtown Vancouver

The city's bustling commercial core with modern skyscrapers, major shopping districts, cultural venues, and world-class dining.

About

Downtown Vancouver is the economic and cultural heart of the city, featuring iconic skyscrapers like the Canada Place, historic Granville Street, and major retail destinations including West End and Coal Harbour.

Top Attractions

  • Canada Place: Iconic waterfront complex with cruise ship terminal and conference centre.
  • Vancouver Art Gallery: Premier art museum in North America's largest city.
  • Horseshoe Bay Marina: Luxury marina with shops and restaurants.

Best Times to Visit

  • Weekdays Daytime: Shopping and business district energy
  • Evenings: Fine dining and rooftop bars
  • Year-Round: Canada Place is a year-round attraction
