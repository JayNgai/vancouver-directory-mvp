The site

About this directory

A plain listing of places in Vancouver, with a planner that turns them into a walking day. No scores, no sponsored placement, no badges.

What this is

Vancouver Directory lists 2,958 places across 23 districts, plus 26 itineraries built from them. The listings are gathered from public data rather than paid placement, which is why the order is plain: there is no ranking this site could justify.

How the planner works

The concierge reads what you type, matches it against categories, neighbourhoods and opening hours, then orders stops by distance and checks the time. It runs on rules, not a language model, so it cannot invent a restaurant. When it says a place opens at 10:30, that came from the listing. You can swap any stop for one of the alternatives it offers, and export the route to Google Maps or Apple Maps.

What this site does not show

Ratings, review counts, prices, and "verified" labels. Free data cannot confirm them, and a number nobody can check is worse than no number.

Photographs

A photo captioned "Stand-in photograph" is a licensed stock image, not the venue. That caption appears on every card and every detail page, and it is the only reason a stand-in is allowed here. If you own a listed business, claim the listing and add your own photographs; the stand-in caption goes when a real one replaces it.

Corrections

Listings go stale. Hours change, kitchens close, a place moves two blocks. Every detail page names the data sources it came from, and the claim form is the fastest route to a correction. What the site collects is on the privacy page.
